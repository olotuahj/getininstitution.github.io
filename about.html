Global Ethos International Institute (GETIN)

Global Ethos International Institute (GETIN) is a dynamic non-governmental organization committed to youth mentorship, leadership development, and the cultivation of strong Character, Attitude, and Behavior (CAB) among young people. At the heart of our work is the CAB Mentorship Program, a monthly initiative designed to educate and guide brilliant young individuals across business, politics, governance, climate action, and societal development. By pairing mentees with experienced mentors whose values align with GETIN’s, we create a new generation of ethical, skilled, and visionary leaders. Through strategic partnerships with government bodies, community stakeholders, and international networks, GETIN combines hands-on mentorship, knowledge sharing, and practical engagement—including conferences, workshops, media initiatives, and cultural activities.

We envision a world where ethical values guide leadership, institutions function with integrity, and societies thrive on trust, fairness, and accountability. Our mission is to promote, institutionalize, and operationalize ethics across the public, private, and social sectors through research, capacity building, advocacy, policy advisory, and innovative ethical solutions, while empowering young brilliant minds.

WHAT WE DO »

Ethics & Professional Development

GETIN provides mini-trainings, short courses, and advanced certifications in business ethics, tech ethics, healthcare ethics, environmental ethics, leadership, compliance, and data ethics. Our programs equip young people with practical skills, ethical knowledge, and professional competence.

CAB Mentorship & Leadership

The CAB mentorship program tracks mentee progress through quarterly conferences, public engagement, and publications. Experienced mentors help nurture a new generation of leaders with strong character, attitude, and behaviour.

Global Expansion & Strategic Partnerships

Through strategic partnerships, sponsorships, and professional planning, GETIN is expanding its reach, growing its mentee base, establishing international mentorship centres, and advancing ethical leadership and youth development worldwide.
